About Me

Hi! I'm Pepakayala Sivasai, a passionate and driven B.Tech CSE student at Pragati Engineering College, graduating in 2027. I'm deeply enthusiastic about technology, especially Full-Stack Web Development, AI tools, and real-world problem solving through code.

Over the past year, I've built several impactful projects including CarbonCount, a web-based carbon footprint calculator, a Face Recognition App, and a Hybrid Chatbot. I'm also certified as a Salesforce AI Associate, and continuously learning tools like Tableau, Vertex AI, and Google Cloud.

With hands-on experience in technologies like JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, MongoDB, MySQL, Java, and Python, I enjoy turning ideas into scalable, user-friendly web applications.

When I’m not coding, I’m exploring new tech, participating in hackathons, or working on self-growth through learning platforms and real-world collaborations.
